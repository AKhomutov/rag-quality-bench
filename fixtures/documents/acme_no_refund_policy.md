# Acme Cloud No-Refund Policy

All Acme Cloud subscription payments are final and non-refundable.

Acme Cloud does not issue refunds for initial purchases, subscription renewals, annual subscriptions, unused subscription time, service interruptions, or accidental purchases.

Customers experiencing service outages may be eligible for service credits under the Service Level Agreement, but service credits are not cash refunds and cannot be exchanged for money.

Exceptions to this no-refund policy are not offered.
