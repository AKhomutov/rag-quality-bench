**Summary:** Acme Cloud’s severity model prioritizes urgency from **Severity 0 (emergency)** to **Severity 5 (informational)**. Severity 0 and 1 involve critical service or data risks, with rapid response targets. Higher severities (2–5) address operational or informational issues, with escalating response times. Severity 2.5 bridges major outages (Severity 2) and partial usability (Severity 3), while Severity 4 and 5 focus on non-critical product and informational matters.
